<p>I was a school librarian for eleven years. The best part of the job was the five minutes after the bell, when a student would come back to the desk and say &ldquo;Have you got another one like that?&rdquo; This site is my attempt to keep having that conversation.</p>

  <img src="{{< asset-url "/assets/img/avatar-nora.svg" >}}" width="400" height="400" alt="Portrait placeholder for Nora Vale">
  <figcaption>Me, approximately.</figcaption>
</figure>

<p>I read about sixty books a year. Most of them are literary fiction, quieter fantasy, essays and the occasional slim book of poems. I don&rsquo;t review everything I read; you can see the full list, abandoned books included, in my <a href="{{< relref "/reading-log" >}}">reading log</a>.</p>

<h2>How I rate books</h2>
<ul class="rating-key">
    <li><span class="rating-line"><span class="rating" style="--rating: 5" role="img" aria-label="Rated 5 out of 5"></span><span aria-hidden="true">5</span></span> A book I&rsquo;ll press into people&rsquo;s hands. Rare: about one a year.</li>
    <li><span class="rating-line"><span class="rating" style="--rating: 4" role="img" aria-label="Rated 4 out of 5"></span><span aria-hidden="true">4</span></span> Excellent, with a flaw or two I could name.</li>
    <li><span class="rating-line"><span class="rating" style="--rating: 3" role="img" aria-label="Rated 3 out of 5"></span><span aria-hidden="true">3</span></span> Good company. Glad I read it, unlikely to reread it.</li>
    <li><span class="rating-line"><span class="rating" style="--rating: 2" role="img" aria-label="Rated 2 out of 5"></span><span aria-hidden="true">2</span></span> Something didn&rsquo;t work for me, and I&rsquo;ll say what.</li>
    <li><span class="rating-line"><span class="rating" style="--rating: 1" role="img" aria-label="Rated 1 out of 5"></span><span aria-hidden="true">1</span></span> I finished it so you don&rsquo;t have to. I almost never post these.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Review copies</h2>
<p>I&rsquo;m happy to receive review copies, but I can&rsquo;t promise a review, and I&rsquo;ll always say when a book was sent to me. I don&rsquo;t accept payment for reviews, and I don&rsquo;t run sponsored posts.</p>

<blockquote class="quote">
  <p>&ldquo;A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies. The man who never reads lives only one.&rdquo;</p>
</blockquote>
<p class="muted"><small>A line I had pinned above the library desk for years. I&rsquo;ve since stopped worrying about who said it first.</small></p>
