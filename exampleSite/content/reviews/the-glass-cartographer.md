The ship has been travelling for three hundred years and its corridors have started to rearrange themselves. Tamsin is the only person who still makes maps, and nobody believes hers.

The ideas are big, but the book is built from small scenes: a shared meal, a borrowed coat, an argument about a door. The ending is hopeful, and it earns it.
